Who I Am

I'm Veasna Prim, a Data Science student at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia (ITC), driven by a passion for transforming data into actionable knowledge. My journey in data science spans analytics, machine learning, and business intelligence — always with the goal of making data speak clearly.

I work across the full data pipeline: from cleaning and exploring datasets with Python and R, to building predictive models with machine learning algorithms, to presenting findings through interactive Power BI dashboards and polished Excel reports that decision-makers can act on.

I'm also deeply interested in computer vision, applying tools like OpenCV and YOLO to real-world challenges — including my corn disease detection project that helps Cambodian farmers identify crop issues early.

I believe data has the power to solve real problems, and I'm looking for internship opportunities where I can contribute insights, grow alongside experienced data professionals, and put my analytical skills to work from day one.
